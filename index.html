🪐 Explore the Planets

🌍 Earth

Our home planet and the only world currently known to support life.

🔴 Mars

Known as the Red Planet because of the iron minerals on its surface.

🪐 Saturn

Famous for its spectacular system of rings made mostly of ice and rock.

🔵 Neptune

A distant blue planet with extremely powerful winds.

⭐ Stars

Stars are enormous balls of extremely hot gas that produce light and heat.

🌌 Galaxies

🌌 The Milky Way

Our galaxy contains billions of stars, including our Sun and its planetary system.

A galaxy is a huge collection of stars, planets, gas, dust and other matter held together by gravity.
